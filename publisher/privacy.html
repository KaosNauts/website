Privacy policy

KaosNauts Media Publisher, including its Instagram component KaosNauts Media Publisher-IG.

Who operates the tool

The KaosNauts team operates this internal publishing tool for its authorized social accounts. Questions and requests can be sent to kaosnauts@gmail.com.

Information accessed and stored

  • OAuth access and refresh credentials and app credentials needed to connect the authorized accounts. Account passwords are entered on the platform's own login pages; the publisher does not collect them.
  • Account and channel identifiers, usernames, profile information and media or video status needed to verify the destination and track publication.
  • Media selected by an operator, approved copies of that media, titles, descriptions, captions and scheduled times.
  • Operational records such as upload sessions, remote media identifiers, processing states, results and errors.

Why this information is used

Information is used to identify the intended account, prepare and approve posts, upload or publish approved content, renew authorized access and resolve publishing failures. Authorized team members may review content and operational records for these purposes.

For Google, the requested permissions are youtube.upload and youtube.readonly. For Instagram, they are instagram_business_basic and instagram_business_content_publish.

Google API data and Limited Use

Google API data is used for the authorized publishing functions described here, in accordance with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

The publisher does not sell account data, use it for advertising targeting, or send it to general-purpose AI services or use it to train AI models.

Storage and sharing

Credentials, queues, account configuration, approved media copies and operational records are stored on the operator's device. Their protection relies on device security and operating-system file permissions. API connections use HTTPS; Google's local authorization callback runs on the operator's loopback interface.

Approved content and the information needed to publish it are sent to the selected platform through its official API. Any configured media host receives the media placed there by the operator.

When Instagram publishing is enabled, approved media must be available at a public HTTPS URL so Meta can retrieve it. Anyone with that URL may access the file while it remains hosted. The operator controls the hosting location and removal of the hosted file.

The social platforms process account information and published content under their own policies: Google privacy policy and Instagram privacy policy.

Retention and deletion

Credentials are retained until replaced, revoked or removed. Local media, queues and operational records remain until an authorized operator removes them. Stop the runner and resolve pending publishing operations before removing their local records.

To request deletion of retained account information, contact kaosnauts@gmail.com. An authorized operator can remove the relevant local credentials, configuration, media and records, and remove media from the configured host.

Revoking access

You can revoke Google's authorization in Google Account connections and Instagram's authorization in Instagram Apps and Websites. The publisher then needs a new authorization before it can continue accessing that account.

Revocation or removal of local files does not itself delete content already published on YouTube or Instagram. That content can be managed through the corresponding platform.

Changes

Changes to data practices will be reflected on this page. Additional account access will require the account owner's authorization.
