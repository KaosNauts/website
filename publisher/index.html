KaosNauts Media Publisher

An internal tool for preparing, approving and scheduling content for the KaosNauts YouTube and Instagram accounts.

For the KaosNauts team. Account owners authorize access through Google or Instagram. Authorized operators review the account, media, text and schedule before enabling publication.

What it does

  • Builds a local queue of approved videos, images and accompanying text.
  • Uploads approved videos and schedules publication through the YouTube Data API.
  • Publishes approved photos and videos through the Instagram API.
  • Keeps local operational records to track processing, failures and uncertain results.

Account access

The YouTube connection requests youtube.upload and youtube.readonly to upload content and verify the channel and video status. Instagram requests instagram_business_basic and instagram_business_content_publish to identify the authorized account and publish its content.

The Instagram component is named KaosNauts Media Publisher-IG. This is part of the same internal publishing tool.

Data and control

Credentials, account configuration, queues, approved media copies and publishing records are stored on the operator's device. Approved content is sent to the selected platform when publishing is enabled. Instagram also needs a publicly accessible HTTPS media URL so Meta can retrieve the approved file.
