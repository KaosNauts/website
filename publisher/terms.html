Usage terms

KaosNauts Media Publisher is an internal tool operated by the KaosNauts team for its authorized social accounts.

Authorized use

Use is limited to account owners and team members authorized to manage KaosNauts content. The tool does not provide public account registration. Operators must have permission to access each connected account and to publish the selected material.

Approval and account controls

Operators review the destination account, media, accompanying text and publication time before approving an item. Approval and enabling publication are explicit steps. Keep access credentials and the device running the publisher under the control of authorized operators.

Account owners may revoke access through the relevant platform. Credentials can expire or be revoked, and some configuration changes require a new authorization.

Publishing and availability

Uploads and posts depend on the social platforms, network connectivity, media hosting, API permissions, quotas and processing. Scheduled publication can be delayed or fail. The publisher records results and uncertain states so an operator can review them; it does not guarantee delivery or exactly-once publication.

Operators are responsible for monitoring results and resolving failed or uncertain operations before manually retrying or publishing the same material elsewhere.

Connected platforms

Use of the connected services remains subject to their terms and policies, including the YouTube Terms of Service and Instagram Terms of Use.

Privacy and support

The privacy policy explains account access, local storage, media hosting, retention and deletion. Contact kaosnauts@gmail.com for account-access or publishing questions.
